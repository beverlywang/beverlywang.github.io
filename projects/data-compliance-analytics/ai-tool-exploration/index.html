AI Tool Exploration

Changing how the design team works

The team had just finished transitioning from Sketch to Figma two years earlier — reluctance around tool changes was already baked into the culture. AI was new and nobody was mentally ready for it.

As the sole designer on a project demanding fast exploration across a zero-to-one dashboard, a full application migration, and design system contributions simultaneously, I couldn't wait for the team to get comfortable.

I needed to find what worked myself first and bring the team along without triggering the same resistance that came with the last transition.
