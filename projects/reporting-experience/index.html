Reporting Experience (AI)

Summary

Challenge The reporting tool in Alta has a very complicated past because it used to be a stand-alone product owned by Aptare but Veritas bought the company so later on it was integrated into Veritas Alta without much changes. In addition to that, the tool itself was quite difficult to use and it required steep learning curve.

Role & Team Lead Designer; collaborated with Product Management, Engineering, UX Research.

Solution & Outcome Designed AI-driven reporting concepts and drove product discussion with stakeholders (PM & Engineering leads). I collaborated with Engineering to create a working Proof of Concept (POC). It was shared with the largest customer (They were very impressed and asked when can they have it). It was also shared in an All-Employee meeting. It will be released soon.

The challenge

The reporting tool in Alta has a very complicated past because it used to be a stand-alone product owned by Aptare but Veritas bought the company so later on it was integrated into Veritas Alta without much changes. In addition to that, the tool itself was quite difficult to use and it required steep learning curve.

There is an opportunity to revamp the reporting experience.

Let's dream big

As a UX designer, of course I want to make IT admin's work easier. It should not be difficult to create, modify, share and use reports. As part of the ideation effort, we brought in IDEO to help us re-imagine the Alta Experience (Cloud data management platform). Monitoring and reporting was one of the themes. I contributed and participated the entire design process.

Balance UX and Engineering conflicts

The concepts were great because they were truly built on user insights from user interviews. The visual styling was also modern. However, the amount of engineering effort required to implement would be massive. There was no enough time and resources to revamp everything in near term. In addition to that, introducing drastic change to an old product can be alarming to existing users who have been using the product for years.

So we need to be cognizant about introducing new changes.

Can we find a win-win solution in which IT admins would be much happier and it doesn't require too much engineering effort so that we can still make a big impact in short term?

Bridge the gap between the dream and reality

As part of the effort to bring concepts closer to what we can deliver soon, I had digested all information from previous design, research and discussions. I also audited the current reporting experience in detail. Moreover, I looked into competitor's reporting tools and chatbot-type of report interaction or inspiration.

Based on those information, I found out that users rely on templates a lot when creating new reports. So having a rich library of templates can be useful. In addition to that, AI chatbot is also a great way to generate reports quickly. It does not require IT admins to understand how to use the reporting tool at all. They just have to write exactly what report they need: Natural Language Processing (NLP) makes their job much easier when the result is accurate.

Copilot AI Assistant Interactions

After discussing within the team, I narrowed down the design focus on AI assistant interactions because most of us agreed that adding AI into the entire product can elevate the experience much better. It's high impact and it requires much less development effort comparing to revamping the entire product. So I decided to explore different ways how AI can help IT admins make better decisions and generate reports within the product.

Idea 1: Reports are generated and shown within chat window

Expanded / light mode & dark mode.

Idea 2: Hybrid view

Copilot acts as conversation driver but the report shows up on the left. Any changes will be reflected on the left.

Idea 3: Show AI recommendations within content and help users understand better

Outcome

At the end, the decision was made to show all the conversation content within the Chatbot view, as well as allow its contextual insight to provide more information to users. Proof of Concept was implemented. Engineering team is actively working to make visual updates to the Copilot. It will be released soon.
